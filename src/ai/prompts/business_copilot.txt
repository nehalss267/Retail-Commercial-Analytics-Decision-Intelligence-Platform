## Key Definitions

- Revenue = Quantity × UnitPrice (negative for returns)
- Churn = No purchase in 90+ days
- CLV = Predicted future customer value (Gradient Boosting)
- RFM = Recency, Frequency, Monetary scoring (quintiles 1-5)
- AOV = Average Order Value = Total Revenue / Total Orders
- Experiment data is synthetic_scenario, not historical
